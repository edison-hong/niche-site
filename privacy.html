Privacy Policy

Effective November 10, 2026 · Operator: Edison Hong · Contact: eddyheng6@gmail.com

Niche ("the app") helps you save places, plan days out, log trips and collect neighborhood stamps. This page explains what we collect, why, who sees it, and how you delete it.

What we collect

  • Account: your Apple ID identifier (and the email Apple shares, which may be a private relay address) or the email you sign in with.
  • Profile: nickname, home city, preferences from onboarding (vibes, budget, trip style), and an optional profile photo.
  • Your content: places you save, trips you plan or log, notes, stamps, and photos you add to a trip. These sync to your account so they survive a new phone.
  • Contacts (only if you allow it): the app turns the email addresses in your contacts into one-way hashes on your phone and sends only the hashes to see which belong to Niche members. We do not keep your contact list or the hashes you send. We store a one-way hash of your own email so friends can find you.
  • Location (only if you allow it): used on your phone to center the map and show what is nearby. It is not stored on our servers.
  • Photo library (only if you allow it): when you tap "Find my photos" while logging a trip, Niche looks at photos taken on the trip's day, on your phone, and reads where they were taken. Only photos you add to a trip are copied into your account.
  • Photos and videos you choose: for photos added to a trip we read the location and time in the photo to suggest places. For videos you upload, speech and on-screen text are read on your phone.
  • Planner requests: the text you type into the AI planner is sent to our server so it can search for places.
  • Reports and blocks: if you report or block someone, we store that so we can review it and hide them from you.
  • Usage analytics (PostHog): which features are used and basic device information, to improve the app.

Who we share it with

  • Supabase hosts our database, sign-in and photo storage.
  • Apple handles Sign in with Apple and subscriptions.
  • Google Places looks up place names, hours and ratings, and Anthropic (Claude) helps write plans. We send only the planner text and place search needed, not your name or account.
  • PostHog receives analytics events.
  • We do not sell your personal data and we do not show ads.

Your choices

  • Turn off Location, Contacts, Photos or Speech Recognition for Niche in iOS Settings at any time.
  • Delete your account: Settings > Account > Delete account. This removes your profile, saved places, trips, photos, reports you made and your sign-in. We also revoke the Sign in with Apple connection.
  • Cancel a subscription in Settings > Apple Account > Subscriptions.
  • To ask what we hold about you, or to have it corrected, email eddyheng6@gmail.com. If you are in the EU, UK or California you may have additional rights, and we will honor them.

Retention

We keep your data while your account exists and delete it when you delete the account. Backups may take up to 30 days to clear.

Children

Niche is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

We will update this page and the date above when the policy changes.
