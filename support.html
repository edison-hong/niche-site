Support

Need help, found a bug, or want to report something? Email eddyheng6@gmail.com. We aim to reply within two days, and within 24 hours for reports of abusive content.

Common questions

  • Delete my account: Settings > Account > Delete account.
  • Cancel Pro: Settings > Apple Account > Subscriptions.
  • Restore a purchase: open Niche Pro and tap Restore purchases.
  • I never got my sign-in code: check spam, then try again after a minute.
